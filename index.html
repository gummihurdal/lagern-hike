Before the first photograph

The bridge, and then the stairs

Brown Boveri Strasse to Schartenfels · no frames from this stretch

Out along Brown Boveri Strasse, through the old town, then down the Promenadenlift — a public lift that drops 27 metres from Bahnhofplatz to the Limmat promenade — and across the Holzbrücke into Ennetbaden.

The covered bridge is worth the stop. Thirty-nine metres, spanning the river without a single pier in the water, built to a competition-winning design by Blasius Balteschwiler of Laufenburg. It went up in 1809. But the crossing itself has been bridged since 1242, when the first structure replaced a ferry that had been running since the eleventh century and doubled as a toll station — the roads to Basel, Schaffhausen, Zürich and Bern all met at this one gap. Yours was at least the sixth bridge on the spot. Flood, ice and war took the other five. Until 1766 it was the only crossing between Zürich and the mouth of the Limmat at Gebenstorf.

It stands at the narrowest point of the gorge, between the Schlossberg and the Lägern — which is why the crossing has survived eight centuries of being destroyed. And the 1809 span rests on seventeenth-century abutments, stacked from massive blocks of limestone. The same Jurassic limestone that makes up the nine kilometres of ridge ahead. The bridge is built out of the walk.

Then the stairs. A hundred metres of them, steeply up the Felsenegg to Schloss Schartenfels and its restaurant. The crest begins directly behind the castle, past an oversized warning sign.

The first frame comes at 11:08, at 470 m — already in the trees above the castle.

Route: Brown Boveri Strasse → Baden old town → Promenadenlift → Holzbrücke → Ennetbaden → Felsenegg stairs → Schloss Schartenfels → the crest west to east → Lägern Hochwacht (859 m) → Regensberg Dorf (617 m). That's Regensberg in Zürich canton, at the east end of the Lägern, not Regensburg in Bavaria.

The ascent figure is approximate. Published figures for the same traverse in the opposite direction give about +610 m and −660 m; walked this way round, from Baden rather than from Dielsdorf, the larger number becomes the climb — hence ≈650 m.

The 13.5 km and the five hours cover the whole walk, Brown Boveri Strasse to Regensberg.
