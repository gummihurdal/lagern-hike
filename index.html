Lägern

A limestone ridge that runs east out of Baden and gets steadily narrower until you're walking on the edge of it. Baden to Regensberg, the whole crest, on foot.

Scroll

Route: Brown Boveri Strasse → Ennetbaden → Schartenfels → the crest west to east → Lägern Hochwacht (859 m) → Regensberg Dorf (617 m). That's Regensberg in Zürich canton, at the east end of the Lägern, not Regensburg in Bavaria.

The ascent figure is approximate. Published figures for the same traverse in the opposite direction give about +610 m and −660 m; walked this way round, from Baden rather than from Dielsdorf, the larger number becomes the climb — hence ≈650 m.

The 13.5 km and the five hours cover the whole walk, Brown Boveri Strasse to Regensberg. The elevation profile at the top of the page is narrower than that: it's built from the photo EXIF, so it runs from the first frame to the last and stops short of both the approach through Ennetbaden and the descent off the ridge. Where a frame shows coordinates, that's where the camera actually was.
